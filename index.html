CloudForge Academy

Train for a cloud career with a working engineer

A six-month live online program across AWS, Azure, CI/CD, infrastructure as code, Kubernetes, cloud security and AI-assisted operations. You finish with six practical capstone projects and a portfolio you can show employers.

The six-month roadmap

Month 1

Foundations and AWS core

Linux, networking, Git and Python, then AWS core services. Outcome: a secure AWS environment.

Month 2

AWS design and Azure basics

AWS solution design and Azure fundamentals. Outcome: an AWS architecture with an Azure comparison.

Month 3

Terraform, Ansible and CI/CD

Infrastructure as code, configuration management and pipelines with Jenkins and GitHub Actions. Outcome: a reviewed delivery pipeline.

Month 4

Containers and GitOps

Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, GitOps and observability. Outcome: a container workload with a dashboard.

Month 5

Security and resilience

Supply-chain security, backup and recovery, and incident response. Outcome: a secure pipeline and a response drill.

Month 6

AIOps and capstone

AI-assisted operations with human approval, then the integrated capstone. Outcome: a portfolio defence.

This is the proposed month-by-month plan. Exact pacing and weekly hours are agreed for each batch. Download the full program guide (PDF).

Capstone projects

Six projects you can show employers

Every track ends with required submissions and a live demonstration. The sixth project brings the earlier ones together.

1. Highly available three-tier application

On AWS. Fail a web-tier instance, show the service recover, and explain the trade-offs.

2. End-to-end secure delivery platform

Commit a change, pass the security gates, deploy through GitOps and roll back.

3. Secure Azure enterprise application

Deploy to staging, verify Key Vault access and show recovery monitoring.

4. Defense in depth and incident response

Inject a misconfiguration, detect it, block the release and carry out an approved containment.

5. AI-assisted operations and incident triage

Inject an incident. The AI explains the evidence and suggests a fix, and the operator approves a reversible action.

6. Secure, observable cloud delivery platform

From pull request to a production-like release, with a rejected unsafe build, a diagnosed incident, a reviewed rollback and a written postmortem.

What services do you offer?

How do I join the academy?

Choose the academy in the form below. We reply with the fees, the batch details and the next steps for the 9 November cohort.

When are the academy classes?

Live online, Monday to Thursday and Saturday, in 90-minute classes from 8:00 to 9:30 PM Eastern Time (EST). The program is open to students in the USA, Africa and worldwide. Tell us where you are in your enquiry and we will help you plan around your time zone.

Can I join only one course?

Yes. Each of the five tracks can be taken on its own. Students in the full program follow one coordinated pathway across all five.

Is the academy suitable for beginners?

The early weeks introduce Linux, networking, Git and cloud basics. If you lack scripting or operating-system experience, we will give you preparation exercises. Tell us your background in your enquiry and we will tell you honestly whether the program fits.

Does the academy guarantee AWS or Microsoft certification?

No. The AWS architecture track is aligned to SAA-C03 preparation, and the Azure track covers administration and introductory architecture foundations. Exam registration and certification are separate, and we do not promise a job.

Do I need my own AWS or Azure account?

Account requirements and lab credits are confirmed for each batch. You get a cost-conscious lab plan and must follow the budget and cleanup instructions.

Will AI make live changes to infrastructure?

No. Live-changing actions need human approval. Automatic fixes are practised only in isolated simulations or expressly authorized sandbox environments.

Who teaches the academy?

A senior developer and senior DevSecOps engineer with more than 22 years of experience. The academy is led by its founder.

Do you provide recordings, certificates, placement help or exam vouchers?

Availability and terms depend on the batch. Ask us when you enquire, and we will confirm what is included.
